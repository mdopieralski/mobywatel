## Kept in Polish

- **The product name** (`WORKING_NAME`, "Sprawnie"): never translated.
- **"mObywatel"**: only as the name of the app this prototype is _not_, and of the mobywatel.ai challenge.
  Never styled as a logo.
- **Polish acronyms and names**: PESEL, ZUS, KSeF; Odyseusz, Bilkom, Karta MKA, 800 plus; cities, offices and
  voivodeships. Keep the acronym and add a short gloss the first time a screen uses it, where the Polish has
  one (en: "ZUS (social insurance)").
- **The document watermark** "PROTOTYP – nie jest dokumentem", the manifest description, route paths and the
  `/brand-voice` page (English).
- **Fictional data**: names, PESELs, plates and numbers are the same in every language.

## Plural categories (CLDR)

- `en`, `de`: `one`, `other`.
- `uk`, `be`, `lt`: `one`, `few`, `many`, `other`.
- `cs`, `sk`: `one`, `few`, `many` (decimals), `other`.
